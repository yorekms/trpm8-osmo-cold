Theoretical & Biophysical Synthesis

Why Peripheral Cold Coding Is an Electro-Metabolic Osmotic Oscillator

Standalone Computational Biophysics & Open Falsification Framework

1. The Classical & Corneal Paradox

Post-2002 textbooks compressed CMR1/TRPM8 into a scalar rate-coded thermometer, sidelining 70 years of intact-fiber recordings showing endogenous Ca2+-controlled, CO2-inhibited, metabolically coupled bursting, plus focal corneal nerve-terminal recordings (Brock et al., 1998–2019) proving cold endings sit chronically depolarized (≥−40 mV) inside the L-type Cav1.3/Cav1.2 window current.

2. Cutaneous Water-Activity Equivalence

Because dcosm/daw = −(Mwaw)−1 ≈ −55.8 Osm/kg at physiological hydration, an intact stratum corneum + SG2 claudin-1/4 seal (Kirschner et al., 2013) blocking 99.8% of ambient humidity drop still delivers +55.8 to +89.3 mOsm/kg of thermodynamic water-activity equivalence to SG-pruned human intraepidermal TRPM8+ terminals (Weyer-Menkhoff et al., 2019)—matching the >340 mOsm/kg corneal threshold (Parra et al., 2014).

3. Unified Genetics & Pharmacology

With one invariant parameter set across all 29 regimes, the 5-ODE model reproduces isothermal +98 mOsm bursting at 33 °C, H+-insensitive menthol tonic lock-in, human TRPM8 (D665N/R30Q, rs10166942), SCN10A (I206M/G1662S), and MICU1 (MPXPS) dynamics, diabetic MCU-KO protection, and dermal dihydropyridine perfusion confounds.

Intact Nerve-Terminal Electrophysiology & Ultrastructure

Five Empirical Pillars from James A. Brock's Guinea-Pig & Mouse Corneal Corpus (1998–2019)

Bridging focal suction-pipette nerve terminal impulses (NTIs) to femtoliter mitochondrial Ca2+/H+ coupling

1. Prolonged Holding & L-Type Window Ca2+

Passing 30–60 s constant polarizing currents across focal corneal NTI seals (Brock et al., 1998, 2001; Carr, Pianova & Brock, 2002, J Gen Physiol) proved cold endings sit chronically depolarized (≥−40 to −30 mV) at warm rest—parking the membrane directly inside the subthreshold window inward Ca2+ current of neuronal Cav1.3 and DHP-sensitive Cav1.2 (Schäfer et al., 1991; Ferroni et al., 1996; Scroggs & Fox, 1992; Xu & Lipscombe, 2001).

2. Single-Terminal L-Type & Synergistic H+ Reset

Confocal Ca2+ imaging inside single intact corneal terminals proved electrically evoked Ca2+ entry is dominated by nifedipine/diltiazem-sensitive L-type VGCCs (Gover, Kao & Weinreich, 2003), while electroneutral PMCA Ca2+/H+ extrusion (Gover et al., 2007; Hwang et al., 2011) and >300 nM mitochondrial TMBIM5–MICU1 Ca2+/H+ antiport (Werth & Thayer, 1994; Austin et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2025) synergistically acidify the cytosol to inhibit firing.

3. Intraburst Tapering & Mobile Initiation

Across 121 guinea-pig corneal cold units, Carr et al. (2003) and Carr, Pianova, McKemy & Brock (2009) proved cyclical terminal burst pacemaking, progressive intraburst NTI amplitude tapering, directional dV/dt hysteresis, burst-to-tonic bifurcation (<30 °C), and mobile initiation loci—matching slow Nav1.8 inactivation (Blair & Bean, 2002, 2003) in Figure 3B.

4. Mitochondria-Packed TRPM8+ Bulbar Endings

Ivanusic, Wood & Brock (2013) and Alamri, Wood, Ivanusic & Brock (2018) proved across mouse and guinea-pig cornea that functionally identified TRPM8+ cold receptors—segregated from ramifying TRPV1+ and PIEZO2+ endings (Bron et al., 2014)—terminate in large superficial bulbar "complex endings" packed with dense mitochondria (Hensel et al., 1974; Heppelmann et al., 2001).

5. Subtype-Selective Mouse Diabetic Loss

Alamri, Brock et al. (2019, IOVS) proved in mouse cornea that high-fat diet and STZ diabetes preferentially destroy TRPM8+ cold complex endings over quiescent TRPV1+ polymodal endings—directly validating our chronic matrix Ca2+ overload prediction alongside Yorek MS et al. (2016) and George et al. (2022, MCU KO).

6. >340 mOsm/kg Mouse Osmosensory Threshold

Adapting Brock's NTI rig to mouse eyes, Parra, Gonzalez-Gonzalez, Gallar & Belmonte (2014, Pain) proved tear hyperosmolality linearly accelerates cold-receptor bursting right above 340 mOsm/kg (+40 mOsm, matching our +42 to +89 mOsm/kg cutaneous Table 2 window!) while polymodal nociceptors require ≥600 mOsm/kg.

1 Stratum Corneum & Vascular Buffering Hydrophobic SC + SG2 tight junctions & dermal perfusion (~290 mOsm/kg) block >99.95% of surface Δaw. Protocol H2-Gel (Zero-Evaporation Submersion, Jevap ≡ 0): Paired Dry vs. Hydrated hands into identical 22.0 °C hydrogel (IR-T matched ±0.2 °C); plus 600–900 mOsm NaCl & mannitol vs. 300 mOsm gel. Dry-preconditioned or hyperosmotic-submerged skin feels equal or warmer than hydrated/isoosmotic controls (p > 0.05, dz < 0.2). N = 15–21 paired
2 Pure Evaporative Cooling Dry-air cold excess is 100% latent heat loss (Lv ≈ 2.45 MJ/kg). Protocol H2 (Paired-Forearm Vapor Occlusion): Petrolatum or Tegaderm barrier on one dry forearm vs. bare contralateral forearm, IR-T matched. Vapor occlusion eliminates the entire perceived cold excess once IR surface temperatures match. N = 15–20 paired
3 Systemic Water-Bolus Sign Paradox Hypoosmolarity inhibits TRPM8 (Quallo 2015); water chills stem from hepatic TRPV4 osmopressor vasoconstriction (Lechner 2011; May 2011). Protocol H4 (Blinded 2×2 Water Crossover + IR Thermography): 500 mL 37 °C water vs. 37 °C saline vs. 15 °C saline vs. 15 °C water, tracking fingertip IR-T & shiver for 30 min. 37 °C water triggers chills only after fingertip IR cooling (or fails when skin T is clamped while 15 °C saline succeeds). N = 16–20 crossover
4 Non-TRPM8 Osmosensor Specificity Hyperosmolar responses stem from TRPV1, TRPV4, TRPA1, ASICs, or PIEZO2 mechanonociceptors (Bron, Wood, Brock & Ivanusic, 2014). Mannitol vs. NaCl (<600 mOsm/kg below polymodal threshold; Parra 2014) + topical TRPM8 blockade/desensitization; plus avascular cornea (−)-(S) vs. (+)-(R)-Bay K 8644 + HC-030031. Effect occurs only with NaCl (not mannitol) or survives complete TRPM8 blockade/desensitization. N = 12–16 / ex vivo
5 Pure Labeled-Line & Temporal Coding Qualia differences (cornea vs. skin; cool vs. wet/shiver) reflect central projections only, not H+-clocked burst structure. Protocols H1 & H3: Isothermal 34 °C hypertonic puffs across V1 territories; H3A grip acidosis comparing menthol vs. 24 °C cooling; H3B 0.03 Hz sinusoidal vs. steady 24 °C Peltier. Tissue acidosis affects menthol and cold identically, AND 0.03 Hz oscillatory vs. steady cooling produce indistinguishable wet/chill qualia. N = 15–20 paired
6 Mitochondrial H+ vs. PI(4,5)P2 Clock Burst reset is driven purely by Ca2+-PLCδ depletion of PI(4,5)P2 (Rohacs 2005; Yudin 2011; Olivares 2015; Yin 2024), PMCA H+ import alone (Hwang 2011), or KCa rather than mitochondrial H+. Cellular DRG/TG Patch-Clamp & Corneal NTI: Simultaneous pHi + PIP2 imaging with heavy cytosolic pH clamping (50–100 mM HEPES) vs. intracellular diC8-PIP2, alongside Ru360 (Austin 2022) vs. PMCA blockade (Gover 2007; Hwang 2011) during prolonged low-drive cold vs. menthol. Clamping pHi fails to alter cold/osmotic oscillatory reset while PIP2 abolishes adaptation identically for cold and menthol. n = 12–20 cells/arm
7 Ecological & Neurovascular Negligibility Ambient VPD (0.3–2.2 kPa) is negligible in intact skin; clinical DHP effects are 100% vascular warming.
