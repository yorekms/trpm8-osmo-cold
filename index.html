Post-2002 textbooks compressed CMR1/TRPM8 into a scalar rate-coded thermometer, sidelining 70 years of intact-fiber recordings showing endogenous Ca2+-controlled, CO2-inhibited, metabolically coupled bursting, plus focal corneal nerve-terminal recordings (Brock et al., 1998–2019) proving cold endings sit chronically depolarized (≥−40 mV) inside the L-type Cav1.3/Cav1.2 window current.

Intact Nerve-Terminal Electrophysiology & Ultrastructure

Five Empirical Pillars from James A. Brock's Guinea-Pig & Mouse Corneal Corpus (1998–2019)

Bridging focal suction-pipette nerve terminal impulses (NTIs) to femtoliter mitochondrial Ca2+/H+ coupling

1. Prolonged Holding & L-Type Window Ca2+

Passing 30–60 s constant polarizing currents across focal corneal NTI seals (Brock et al., 1998, 2001; Carr, Pianova & Brock, 2002, J Gen Physiol) proved cold endings sit chronically depolarized (≥−40 to −30 mV) at warm rest—parking the membrane directly inside the subthreshold window inward Ca2+ current of neuronal Cav1.3 and DHP-sensitive Cav1.2 (Schäfer et al., 1991; Ferroni et al., 1996; Scroggs & Fox, 1992; Xu & Lipscombe, 2001).

2. Single-Terminal L-Type & Synergistic H+ Reset

Confocal Ca2+ imaging inside single intact corneal terminals proved electrically evoked Ca2+ entry is dominated by nifedipine/diltiazem-sensitive L-type VGCCs (Gover, Kao & Weinreich, 2003), while electroneutral PMCA Ca2+/H+ extrusion (Gover et al., 2007; Hwang et al., 2011) and >300 nM mitochondrial TMBIM5–MICU1 Ca2+/H+ antiport (Werth & Thayer, 1994; Austin et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2025) synergistically acidify the cytosol to inhibit firing.

3. Intraburst Tapering & Mobile Initiation

Across 121 guinea-pig corneal cold units, Carr et al. (2003) and Carr, Pianova, McKemy & Brock (2009) proved cyclical terminal burst pacemaking, progressive intraburst NTI amplitude tapering, directional dV/dt hysteresis, burst-to-tonic bifurcation (<30 °C), and mobile initiation loci—matching slow Nav1.8 inactivation (Blair & Bean, 2002, 2003) in Figure 3B.

4. Mitochondria-Packed TRPM8+ Bulbar Endings

Ivanusic, Wood & Brock (2013) and Alamri, Wood, Ivanusic & Brock (2018) proved across mouse and guinea-pig cornea that functionally identified TRPM8+ cold receptors—segregated from ramifying TRPV1+ and PIEZO2+ endings (Bron et al., 2014)—terminate in large superficial bulbar "complex endings" packed with dense mitochondria (Hensel et al., 1974; Heppelmann et al., 2001).

5. Subtype-Selective Mouse Diabetic Loss

Alamri, Brock et al. (2019, IOVS) proved in mouse cornea that high-fat diet and STZ diabetes preferentially destroy TRPM8+ cold complex endings over quiescent TRPV1+ polymodal endings—directly validating our chronic matrix Ca2+ overload prediction alongside Yorek MS et al. (2016) and George et al. (2022, MCU KO).

6. >340 mOsm/kg Mouse Osmosensory Threshold

Adapting Brock's NTI rig to mouse eyes, Parra, Gonzalez-Gonzalez, Gallar & Belmonte (2014, Pain) proved tear hyperosmolality linearly accelerates cold-receptor bursting right above 340 mOsm/kg (+40 mOsm, matching our +42 to +89 mOsm/kg cutaneous Table 2 window!) while polymodal nociceptors require ≥600 mOsm/kg.

Dialectical Biophysics & Rival Literature Synthesis (Manuscript Section 4.2)

Six Competing Mechanistic Alternatives & Quantitative Rebuttals

Steelmanning mainstream rival frameworks across barrier physics, ion-channel pacemaking, PIP2, neuropathy, genetics & clinical trials
Alternative 1 • Skin Barrier & Hygrosensation

Isotonic Epidermal Equilibrium & Pure Evaporative Cooling

Rival View: TEWL flattens across 0–80% RH (Blank et al., 1984); SC–SG water jumps reflect lipid partitioning with Na+/K+/Cl− near dermal equilibrium (Warner 1988; von Zglinicki 1993); SG2 claudin-1/4 blocks ions > water (Furuse 2002; Kirschner 2013); outer SG hosts Mrgprd+ nociceptors (Dussor 2008); wetness is pure evaporative cooling + touch (Filingeri 2014).

Rebuttal: An ion-blocking, water-permeable claudin seal is the exact prerequisite for trans-membrane μw suction (−55.8 Osm/kg·Δaw) without a salt lake—proven in vivo because dry air induces outer-SG TAUT/NFAT5/AQP3/osmolyte transcription 3–5× (Janeke 2003; Grafe 2004; Warskulat 2004; Zijl 2025), barrier loss collapses SG PIXE gradients (Mauro 1998), and human epidermal TRPM8—not TRPA1—tracks cooling (Hilliges 1995; Weyer-Menkhoff 2019).

Alternative 2 • Electrical Pacemaking

Ih/HCN1 + Kv1/K2P/Kv7 Brakes + T-Type Cav3 Alone

Rival View: Burst timing and threshold are set purely by HCN1 (Ih; Orio 2009, 2012) vs. IKD (Madrid 2009), TREK/TRAAK/TASK-3 (Noël 2009; Morenilla-Palao 2014; Pereira 2014), Kv7 (Abd-Elsayed 2015), SK2 (Maingret 2008), and Nav1.8-negative K+ brakes (Luiz 2019; Olivares 2015); somatic L-type is HVA while T-type is subthreshold (Fox 1987; Rozanski 2013), and Mikulec (1995) titled diltiazem as "sparing" cold fibers.

Rebuttal: Somatic clamps (−70 mV) miss chronic terminal depolarization (≥−40 to −30 mV; Carr, Pianova & Brock 2002), where T-type Cav3 is steady-state inactivated while neuronal Cav1.3 and interspike DRG L-type channels open (Schäfer 1991; Ferroni 1996; Xu & Lipscombe 2001). Single-terminal corneal Ca2+ is blocked by nifedipine/diltiazem (Gover 2003); inside Mikulec (1995), 10–250 μM diltiazem depressed cold fibers while Ni2+ did not; and Kv7 block spares corneal cold thermoreceptors (Vetter 2013).

Alternative 3 • Adaptation Clock

Ca2+-PLCδ PI(4,5)P2 Depletion & Linear PMCA H+ Import

Rival View: TRPM8 desensitizes via Ca2+-PLCδ hydrolysis of PI(4,5)P2 (Liu & Qin 2005; Rohacs 2005; Daniels 2009; Yudin 2011; Yin 2024; Kim 2026), while trigeminal/HeLa Ca2+ acidification arises from plasma-membrane PMCA Ca2+/2H+ import rather than mitochondria (Hwang 2011; Poburko 2011; Behrendt 2004; Kühn 2009; Shao 2016).

Rebuttal: PIP2 depletion desensitizes cold and menthol symmetrically (Yin 2024) and requires zero ΔΨm, failing to explain: (i) intracellular H+ blocking cold/icilin while sparing menthol (Andersson 2004; menthol stabilizes PIP2 in Kim 2026); (ii) mitochondrial poison block of DRG acidification (Werth & Thayer 1994); and (iii) why linear low-load PMCA (<300 nM; Gover 2007) damps monotonically without the steep >300 nM cooperative MICU1/MCU/TMBIM5 relaxation threshold (Austin 2022; Zhang 2025).

Alternative 4 • Neuropathic Cold Allodynia

Silent Nav1.8+ Nociceptors, Corneal TRPV1 & Hemoglobin/TRPA1 Acidosis

Rival View: Neuropathic cold allodynia recruits silent Trpm8-negative Nav1.8+ nociceptors via Kv1 loss (MacDonald 2021), oxaliplatin hemoglobin proton-buffering acidosis activating TRPA1 (reversed by acetazolamide; Potenzieri 2020), or corneal TRPV1/substance P co-release (Li F et al. 2019), while diabetic skin fiber loss correlates weakly with pain (Sorensen 2006).

Rebuttal: Bulk acidosis inhibits TRPM8 while activating TRPA1—cleanly separating noxious (<15 °C) TRPA1/silent-nociceptor pain from cool/evaporative TRPM8 dysesthesia (20–28 °C) prevented in vivo by nifedipine/diltiazem via L-type/NFAT (Gauchan 2009; Kono 2012; Kawashiri 2012). Early ΔΨm H+-brake loss causes hyperexcitable firing before axon loss (resolving Sorensen 2006), followed by matrix Ca2+ overload destroying active TRPM8+ complex endings ahead of quiescent TRPV1+ fibers (Alamri, Brock et al. 2019).

Alternative 5 • Human Genetics

SNP Meta-Analysis Noise, Menthol Dry-Eye Parity & Silent MICU1 Charts

Rival View: Heterozygous rs10166942 meta-analyses show ancestry heterogeneity (Siokas 2022; cf. Soeda 2021); menthol cold thresholds are unchanged in dry eye (Kaido 2021); SCN10A SFN cohorts (3.7% of 1,139 cases; Han 2014; Eijkenboom 2019) favor warmth pain; and MICU1/TMBIM5 myopathy charts (Kohlschmidt 2021; Zhang 2022) lack cold complaints.

Rebuttal: Normal menthol threshold with heightened unpleasantness in dry eye (Kaido 2021) is a direct positive confirmation of Figure 3H, because menthol bypasses hyperosmolar gating and H+ adaptation! Coding GoF mutations in TRPM8 and SCN10A reproducibly trigger post-LASIK/cooling pain, and Table 4 predicts MICU1 LoF causes −91% collapsed cold-burst amplitude (blunted adaptation, matching Kohlschmidt 2021)—not spontaneous pain.

Alternative 6 • Clinical Pharmacology

Modest Raynaud's CCB Trials & Acoltremon Sign–Symptom Split

Rival View: Oral CCBs show modest primary Raynaud's benefit (SMD 0.23; Ennis 2016; Rirash 2017) attributed purely to vasodilation (cf. diabetic rat ACEi/ARB perfusion in Coppey 2006), while acoltremon met Phase 3 COMET-2/3 Schirmer tearing endpoints (N = 931; Pattar 2026; Lee 2025) after mixed Phase 2b/symptom splits (Yoon 2021; Wirta 2022).

Rebuttal: Figure 5B quantitatively predicts compressed systemic DHP sensory effects because vascular Cav1.2 warming/washout opposes incomplete block of 20× less sensitive neuronal Cav1.3 (Xu & Lipscombe 2001) and off-target TRPA1 activation (Fajardo 2008)—requiring avascular corneal or IR-clamped DHP vs. ACEi/ARB tests—while synthetic TRPM8 agonism drives reflex tearing even as peripheral terminals cross burst-to-tonic accommodation (Carr 2003).

Seven Pre-Specified Popperian Falsification Criteria

# Failure Mode / Kill-Switch Competing Rival Hypothesis Crucial Decisive Test Refutation Criterion (Model Falsified If...) Target N
1 Stratum Corneum & Vascular Buffering Hydrophobic SC + SG2 tight junctions & dermal perfusion (~290 mOsm/kg) block >99.95% of surface Δaw. Protocol H2-Gel (Zero-Evaporation Submersion, Jevap ≡ 0): Paired Dry vs. Hydrated hands into identical 22.0 °C hydrogel (IR-T matched ±0.2 °C); plus 600–900 mOsm NaCl & mannitol vs. 300 mOsm gel. Dry-preconditioned or hyperosmotic-submerged skin feels equal or warmer than hydrated/isoosmotic controls (p > 0.05, dz < 0.2). N = 15–21 paired
2 Pure Evaporative Cooling Dry-air cold excess is 100% latent heat loss (Lv ≈ 2.45 MJ/kg). Protocol H2 (Paired-Forearm Vapor Occlusion): Petrolatum or Tegaderm barrier on one dry forearm vs. bare contralateral forearm, IR-T matched. Vapor occlusion eliminates the entire perceived cold excess once IR surface temperatures match. N = 15–20 paired
3 Systemic Water-Bolus Sign Paradox Hypoosmolarity inhibits TRPM8 (Quallo 2015); water chills stem from hepatic TRPV4 osmopressor vasoconstriction (Lechner 2011; May 2011). Protocol H4 (Blinded 2×2 Water Crossover + IR Thermography): 500 mL 37 °C water vs. 37 °C saline vs. 15 °C saline vs. 15 °C water, tracking fingertip IR-T & shiver for 30 min. 37 °C water triggers chills only after fingertip IR cooling (or fails when skin T is clamped while 15 °C saline succeeds). N = 16–20 crossover
4 Non-TRPM8 Osmosensor Specificity Hyperosmolar responses stem from TRPV1, TRPV4, TRPA1, ASICs, or PIEZO2 mechanonociceptors (Bron, Wood, Brock & Ivanusic, 2014). Mannitol vs. NaCl (<600 mOsm/kg below polymodal threshold; Parra 2014) + topical TRPM8 blockade/desensitization; plus avascular cornea (−)-(S) vs. (+)-(R)-Bay K 8644 + HC-030031. Effect occurs only with NaCl (not mannitol) or survives complete TRPM8 blockade/desensitization. N = 12–16 / ex vivo
5 Pure Labeled-Line & Temporal Coding Qualia differences (cornea vs. skin; cool vs. wet/shiver) reflect central projections only, not H+-clocked burst structure. Protocols H1 & H3: Isothermal 34 °C hypertonic puffs across V1 territories; H3A grip acidosis comparing menthol vs. 24 °C cooling; H3B 0.03 Hz sinusoidal vs. steady 24 °C Peltier. Tissue acidosis affects menthol and cold identically, AND 0.03 Hz oscillatory vs. steady cooling produce indistinguishable wet/chill qualia. N = 15–20 paired
6 Mitochondrial H+ vs. PI(4,5)P2 Clock Burst reset is driven purely by Ca2+-PLCδ depletion of PI(4,5)P2 (Rohacs 2005; Yudin 2011; Olivares 2015; Yin 2024), PMCA H+ import alone (Hwang 2011), or KCa rather than mitochondrial H+. Cellular DRG/TG Patch-Clamp & Corneal NTI: Simultaneous pHi + PIP2 imaging with heavy cytosolic pH clamping (50–100 mM HEPES) vs. intracellular diC8-PIP2, alongside Ru360 (Austin 2022) vs. PMCA blockade (Gover 2007; Hwang 2011) during prolonged low-drive cold vs. menthol. Clamping pHi fails to alter cold/osmotic oscillatory reset while PIP2 abolishes adaptation identically for cold and menthol. n = 12–20 cells/arm
7 Ecological & Neurovascular Negligibility Ambient VPD (0.3–2.2 kPa) is negligible in intact skin; clinical DHP effects are 100% vascular warming. Dew-Point Mobile EMA (Web App) + Chronic DHP vs. ACEi/ARB Protocol H2-Gel: Within-subject βVPD|T + IR-clamped DHP vs. ACEi/ARB users with PPG perfusion covariate. Within-subject βVPD|T ≈ 0 (p > 0.05) after air-T & occlusion control, and DHP vs. ACEi/ARB show zero difference once skin T & PPG match. N ≥ 20 EMA; 20/arm
